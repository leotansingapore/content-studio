# Postmosis is listed in the free directories buyers browse

Status: todo
App Postmosis (postmosis.com), owner Leo + Claude, phase 4 (Named and cited).

## Why

Listings bring a new domain its first outside links, and the rivals lean on theirs. Supergrow's homepage leads with "4.8/5 on G2", Predis.ai shows 4.7/5 on G2, Taplio's one Product Hunt launch was #1 of the day with 1,008 upvotes, and AuthoredUp's Chrome extension has 30,000 users and a Featured badge (teardown.md, 30 Oct 2026).

Some listings need what Postmosis lacks today. G2 and Capterra need customers who will write reviews, and a Product Hunt launch needs a self-serve path, because launch visitors would meet a booking page (teardown.md, items 23 and 24).

## What we have today

Checked 30 Oct 2026 against the teardown, product-truth.md and the live site.

- No directory listings. The teardown found "no affiliate programme, ads or directory listings".
- No open sign-up: a customer gets an account through a walkthrough with Leo (product-truth.md, fact 1), so the site's one action is Book a walkthrough.
- Nothing to show as proof: no customer counts, logos, quotes, ratings or results exist (product-truth.md, Not claimable).
- postmosis.com went live on 29 Oct 2026, and Google had indexed 96 of its 113 sitemap URLs by 30 Oct (URL Inspection).

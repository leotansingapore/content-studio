## How the best do it

- MagicSlides' founder (Starter Story, youtu.be/X2T92MVx7xs) runs about 30 small products. On Instagram he recorded ten videos in about an hour, each him holding his phone and saying what one app does, added a title and caption in Instagram's Edits app, and posted. He did it as an experiment because he did not know which would work, and kept posting (12:15).
- On 9 Nov 2026 his Instagram, @sanskarr.tiwari, had 66K followers.
- Canary's founder runs the same test as his growth loop: post a lot, find the winner, make variations (youtu.be/KgS3hbpxTzE at 7:12).

A coach, an adviser or a consultant sells several services and rarely knows which one people want to hear about. Ten short takes in one sitting answer that in a week.

## What we have today

- Brand lists the products and services scanned from a user's site, each with Write a post about it (brand-products).
- Batch turns one topic into one post per platform.
- Film it is a teleprompter that records a front-camera take into the video editor (formats-hooks-scripts, Leo's sign-ins).
- Analytics' What wins for you sets format, hook and angle against your usual (learn-format-winners).
- Nothing writes one script per service at once, and nothing ranks results by the service a post was about.

## Build

1. A Ten takes option in Write. It reads the services from Brand, or takes a typed list of up to ten.
2. It writes one 20-second script per service, spoken to camera: who it is for, what you do, one line asking them to message you.
3. Film it plays the scripts back to back in one sitting. Each take saves as its own draft, tagged with its service.
4. A week after they post, Analytics ranks the services by views and messages against your usual, and the top one gets Make another.
5. One writing call per script on Write's caps. Every script runs the compliance check, and an adviser's script names a service, never a product.

## Done when

- A user with services in Brand gets one script per service and can film them in one sitting.
- Each take is a draft tagged with its service.
- Analytics ranks services a week after posting.

## Tests

- One script per listed service, never more than ten.
- Each saved take carries its service tag.
- The ranking compares each service's posts with the user's usual, not with each other's raw numbers.

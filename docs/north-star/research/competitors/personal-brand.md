Researched 2026-10-19 for Content Studio. 22 competitors, 198 sourced features. Machine-readable version: personal-brand.json (same folder).

Method: vendor sites, pricing pages, help centers and changelogs via WebFetch; third-party reviews only where the vendor page was silent, and marked as such. Anything not confirmed by a source is marked unverified.

## Taplio

- URL: https://taplio.com
- Positioning: The 'full loop' LinkedIn growth tool: viral library, AI writing in your voice, scheduling, engagement feed and lead outreach in one app.
- Audience: LinkedIn creators, founders, B2B sales and ghostwriters (12k+ creators and teams claimed).
- Pricing: Starter $39/mo, Growth $69/mo, Pro $199/mo billed monthly (shown as '$', USD assumed). The same page's comparison table shows $32 / $49 / $149, unlabeled; Taplio's own Sep 2026 blog gives Starter $32/mo and Growth $49/mo annual. 7-day free trial with full Pro access, 30-day money-back. Multi-seat discount 'Save up to 30%'. AI writing is not on Starter; 250 AI credits + 500 comment credits/mo on Starter/Growth (mapping inferred), unlimited on Pro.

Features:

- **Viral posts 'For You' daily feed** (inspiration): Viral posts > 'For You' tab shows 5 new high-performing LinkedIn posts per day matched to your AI settings. Each card has a 'Why is this viral?' explanation plus 'Save' and 'Remix' buttons. 'Adjust AI' (top right) edits the matching fields: 'You write in', 'You work in', 'You are a', 'You want to reach', 'You post about'. Limits/cost: 5 matches per day. Worth copying: Explaining WHY a post worked on the card itself, before the user remixes it; matching driven by a short profile. Sources: https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17173501-finding-post-inspiration-with-taplio, https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17233357-using-taplio-s-ai-for-content-inspiration
- **Viral library search ('Explore more')** (inspiration): Search bar over a library of 4M+ posts (help center) / '5M+ Inspiration & viral posts' (pricing page). Accepts a topic, idea or full sentence; suggested topic cards run a search in one click. After a search, filters appear: 'Published on', 'Content', 'Creator', 'Keywords', 'Clear all', 'Sort by'. A third tab, 'Saved posts', holds bookmarks. Limits/cost: Library size differs by page (4M+ vs 5M+). Worth copying: Sentence-level semantic search plus suggested topic cards lowers the blank-search problem. Sources: https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17173501-finding-post-inspiration-with-taplio, https://taplio.com/pricing
- **Saved posts and Collections** (inspiration): 'Save' bookmarks any library post into 'Saved posts'; posts can be grouped into Collections. The free Chrome extension adds 'Save post' from inside LinkedIn. Limits/cost: Extension is free and works without a plan. Sources: https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17173501-finding-post-inspiration-with-taplio, https://taplio.com/pricing
- **Remix a viral post** (remix): Flow: pick a post in 'For You' or 'Explore more' -> click 'Remix' -> a variation starts streaming on the 'Ask Taplio' page -> 'Open in composer' -> refine with 'AI options' or generate an image from the post content -> schedule or publish now. Limits/cost: AI features on Growth and Pro only. Worth copying: Remix lands in a chat surface first, then the composer, so the user can iterate before editing. Sources: https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17173354-how-to-generate-an-ai-variation-of-a-post, https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17173501-finding-post-inspiration-with-taplio
- **'Remix my posts that worked' and /repurpose** (remix): Ask Taplio starter suggestion 'Remix my posts that worked' and the composer slash command '/repurpose' rewrite the user's own past winners into fresh variations in their voice. The Remix button also works on the user's own posts. Limits/cost: Growth/Pro. Worth copying: Recycling your own proven posts is a low-risk remix source. Sources: https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17233357-using-taplio-s-ai-for-content-inspiration
- **AI profile ('Adjust AI') as voice and strategy** (brand): Four-step setup: 'About you' (pulled from LinkedIn, editable), 'Tone of Voice', 'Content pillars', 'Audience'. These settings shape every idea, draft and the daily viral matches. Homepage says Taplio also learns voice from your posts and edits. Worth copying: One short profile feeds inspiration matching, ideas and drafting at once. Sources: https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17233357-using-taplio-s-ai-for-content-inspiration, https://taplio.com/
- **Ask Taplio assistant** (agent): Chat assistant that 'has read every post you wrote' and 'knows what LinkedIn rewards this month'. Starter prompts: 'What's working in my niche', 'What worked this month', 'Remix my posts that worked'. Homepage shows a weekly brief of trends with percentage lifts (e.g. 'Founder-led sales past 1M ARR' +212%) and an action 'Draft a post on trend 2, in your voice'. Limits/cost: Weekly trend brief is 'Request early access' (unverified availability). Worth copying: Trend brief with one-tap 'draft on trend N in my voice' is a proactive next-up pattern. Sources: https://taplio.com/, https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17233357-using-taplio-s-ai-for-content-inspiration
- **Post builder** (format-text): Write & Schedule > Post builder. Tab 'From an idea': enter a topic (or 'Suggest topic'), tick one or more variations (Actionable, Storytelling, Thought-provoking, Promotional), optional Format & tone panel ('Edit'), then 'Generate posts'. Tab 'From a URL': choose 'Article' or 'YouTube Video', paste link, 'Generate'. Limits/cost: Each generated version uses '1 Expert Mode credit'. Worth copying: Generating several angle variants at once (story vs actionable vs promo) lets the user pick rather than prompt. Sources: https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17233357-using-taplio-s-ai-for-content-inspiration
- **Composer AI edits and slash commands** (editor): Composer 'AI Edit' tab: 'Improve this post', 'Rewrite the hook', 'Fix grammar and spelling', or a custom instruction. 'Adjust results' fine-tunes output and 'History' restores earlier versions. Slash commands '/postIdeas' (ideas from profile, recent posts and inspiration) and '/repurpose'. Homepage mockup also shows 'Rewrite hook', 'Make it a story', 'My tone'. Limits/cost: Growth/Pro; 250 AI credits/mo on Starter/Growth (inferred), unlimited on Pro. Worth copying: Version history on AI rewrites protects the user's work. Sources: https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17233357-using-taplio-s-ai-for-content-inspiration, https://taplio.com/, https://taplio.com/pricing
- **Live reach prediction** (analytics): Homepage lists 'Live reach prediction before you publish' in the composer. Limits/cost: Unverified beyond homepage copy. Worth copying: Pre-publish score nudges edits before posting. Sources: https://taplio.com/
- **Carousel Generator** (format-carousel): Write & Schedule > Carousel Generator. Start 'From scratch/blank page', from AI input ('Generate Carousel'), or from a website or YouTube URL (slides filled with highlighted text from the source). Suggested structure: slide 1 hook, body slides, final CTA; 7-10 slides advised. Tabs: 'Content' (images), 'Settings' (text formatting), 'Color Palette' or 'Use custom colors' (hex), 'Preview', drag to reorder, 'Reset'. Finish with 'Save for later' (to 'My Carousels'), 'Create Post' (names it and attaches to composer) or 'Download' (PDF). Third-party: also imports tweets, Reddit posts and images, adds profile picture and handle. Limits/cost: Growth/Pro (AI tools count against AI credits). Limits/cost: Counts against AI credits (250/mo Starter/Growth inferred; unlimited Pro). Sources: https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17173354-how-to-generate-an-ai-variation-of-a-post, https://taplio.com/pricing
- **Queue and posting schedule** (calendar): Actions > 'Edit Post schedule': pick a time and 'Add slot' (advice: at least one slot every day, vary times). Add posts to the next slot in one click. Queue controls: 'Re-queue' (pull scheduled posts up to fill gaps), 'Shuffle' (mix order), 'Edit queue'. Day view by default; icon switches to a monthly calendar where clicking a slot creates a post. Homepage also claims 'Best-time scheduling tuned to your followers'. Worth copying: Shuffle and Re-queue keep a batch-written week evenly spread. Sources: https://taplio.com/pricing, https://intercom.help/taplio/en/collections/19726449-writing-publishing-posts
- **Post Boosts (per-post automations)** (engagement): Composer 'Boosts' tab per post: 'Remove URL previews'; 'Scheduled comment' (e.g. link in first comment, optional delay, saved links); 'Auto Connect' (connection requests to likers/commenters, capped per post); 'Auto Reply' (reply to commenters, optional keyword trigger); 'Auto DM' (personalized DM with first name/company to likers or repliers, usually within an hour); 'Also Publish on X' via Tweet Hunter (next slot or same time, 280-char edit). Limits/cost: Auto DM: 1st-degree connections only, stops 24h after publishing, only first 100 engagers. Auto-reply monitoring 2 days (Starter/Growth, inferred) vs 90 days (Pro). Auto-add likers/commenters 20 per post (inferred) vs unlimited (Pro). Worth copying: Keyword comment -> auto DM a resource is the lead magnet loop, configured per post at scheduling time. 'Smart Replies' suggests replies in four tones: 'Add Value', 'Congratulate', 'Agree', 'Disagree'. Snippets and emojis; 'Like & Skip'; 'Auto-Like' when you reply; 'View on LinkedIn'. 'Search by URL' + 'Retrieve Comments' pulls the last 500 comments of any public post. Limits/cost: Only posts published through Taplio; refresh every ~2 hours ('Refresh' to force). Search by URL is Pro only, 500 comments max. 500 comment credits/mo on Starter/Growth (inferred). Worth copying: Four fixed reply intents make AI comments fast to choose and less generic. Sources: https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17455795-engage-comments-tab, https://taplio.com/pricing
- **Engage: Influencers and Custom lists feeds** (engagement): Engage > 'Influencers' shows recent posts from curated niche lists (e.g. 'Accounting', 'Artificial Intelligence', 'DACH Influencers'); 'Custom lists' shows posts from people in your own Contacts lists. Per post: type a reply, 'Smart Reply' (Add value / Congratulate / Agree / Disagree), Snippet, Emoji; quick actions 'Like', 'Open', 'Copy', 'Skip', 'Like & Skip'. Limits/cost: Not real-time: most posts appear in 30-60 minutes, sometimes hours. Worth copying: A focused feed of chosen people replaces doom-scrolling LinkedIn. Sources: https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17456217-engage-influencers-tab, https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17456526-engage-custom-lists-tab
- **Auto lists of likers and commenters (CRM)** (engagement): Contacts lists 'Liked My Posts' and 'Commented My Posts' fill automatically; both exportable for outreach. Past interactions import gradually over the first week; new posts are fetched about 24h after publishing, then rechecked every few days. Limits/cost: Pacing is deliberate to 'limit the risks for your account'. Worth copying: Engagers become a reusable audience list without manual work. ## Supergrow

- URL: https://www.supergrow.ai
- Positioning: 'AI that writes like you, not for you': LinkedIn content from AI interviews and a voice profile, with team approvals for executive programs.
- Audience: Founders, executives and B2B teams/agencies running several LinkedIn voices.
- Pricing: Pro $39/mo monthly or $31/mo annual (1 account); Teams $199/mo monthly or $189/mo annual (5 accounts); Enterprise custom. 7-day free trial. Third-party pages (Taplio blog Sep 2026, Tooliverse Jun 2026) also list a Starter at $19/mo ($16 annual, 2 PostCast sessions/mo) and Teams at $139/mo for 4 accounts; not on the vendor page fetched 2026-10-19.

Features:

- **Content DNA (voice profile)** (brand): Builds a voice profile from your existing LinkedIn posts, writing samples and profile: vocabulary, tone, opinions, known topics, hooks and pillars. Drafts are generated in that voice. Teams plan keeps a separate Content DNA per team member. Limits/cost: Pro: 1 account; Teams: per member (5 accounts). Worth copying: Capturing OPINIONS and known topics, not just tone, so drafts sound like the person's views. Sources: https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing, https://www.supergrow.ai/, https://tooliverse.ai/tools/supergrow
- **PostCast AI interviews** (format-text): Voice or text conversation with an AI interviewer named 'Alex'. A 10-15 minute session (sources vary: 15-20) asks adaptive questions and turns the stories and takeaways into several LinkedIn drafts in your voice ('a week's worth of LinkedIn content'). Teams can 'Push Postcast interviews to team'. Limits/cost: Unlimited on Pro. Third-party: Starter capped at 2 sessions/mo. Output 3-7 drafts per session (sources disagree). Worth copying: Interview-to-posts removes the blank page for experts who will talk but not write; strong fit for financial advisers. Sources: https://www.supergrow.ai/, https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing, https://tooliverse.ai/tools/supergrow, https://taplio.com/blog/taplio-alternatives
- **Repurposing from YouTube, articles, PDF** (remix): Paste a YouTube video, blog/article or PDF and get 'multiple post formats from a single source' in your voice. Limits/cost: Pro and up. Sources: https://www.supergrow.ai/, https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing
- **Voice to Post** (format-text): Speak an idea; AI turns the voice note into a post in your voice. Sources: https://www.supergrow.ai/, https://www.supergrow.ai/compare/supergrow-vs-authoredup
- **Kanban content board** (calendar): Drag-and-drop Kanban with stages 'Draft', 'Review', 'Approved', 'Scheduled'. Worth copying: Approval stage built into the board, not a separate tool. Sources: https://www.supergrow.ai/, https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing
- **Scheduling, calendar and auto first comment** (calendar): Weekly calendar view with posts at peak times; 'Schedule First Comment' auto-publishes a follow-up comment (CTA, hashtags, context) when the post goes live; reusable snippets. Sources: https://www.supergrow.ai/, https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing, https://www.supergrow.ai/compare/supergrow-vs-authoredup
- **Carousel maker** (format-carousel): Carousel maker included on Pro. Third-party descriptions: drag-and-drop editor, 50+ mobile-first templates, inline click-to-edit text, image library, per-workspace brand kit (logos, colors, fonts), direct publish to LinkedIn, conversion 'in under 30 seconds'; infographic generator also listed. Limits/cost: Pro and up. Template count and brand kit unverified on vendor page. Sources: https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing, https://tooliverse.ai/tools/supergrow
- **Engage with Focus Mode and AI comments** (engagement): 'Engage' gives a curated engagement feed; 'Focus Mode' lets you comment without the LinkedIn feed; AI comment drafts in your voice. Third-party: scheduled auto-comments based on engagement. Limits/cost: Pro and up. Sources: https://www.supergrow.ai/, https://www.supergrow.ai/compare/supergrow-vs-authoredup, https://tooliverse.ai/tools/supergrow
- **Profile analytics and weekly report** (analytics): Dashboard of post performance, engagement and follower growth, AI analysis of what worked, and a weekly briefing that explains results ('Insights & Analytics'). Teams get per-member and team-wide weekly reports. Worth copying: A written weekly explanation, not just charts. Sources: https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing, https://www.supergrow.ai/compare/supergrow-vs-authoredup, https://tooliverse.ai/tools/supergrow
- **Team approvals and org dashboard** (team): Teams plan: 'Feedback & approval workflows' (review chain), 'Organisation dashboard', 'Share inspiration across workspaces', 'Content calendar across all members', 'Push content to all team workspaces', 'Publish on behalf of team members', role-based permissions. Limits/cost: Teams $199/mo (5 accounts); Enterprise for more. Worth copying: Push one piece of content into every member's workspace to adapt in their own voice: an employee-advocacy remix. Sources: https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing, https://www.supergrow.ai/
- **Challenges, leaderboard, Slack** (team): 'Challenges & leaderboard' with streaks and ranks to drive adoption; Slack notifications. Limits/cost: Teams plan. Worth copying: Gamified posting streaks for teams of advisers. Sources: https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing, https://www.supergrow.ai/
- **Supergrow MCP** (integrations): Connects Claude or ChatGPT to draft posts, pull analytics and manage teams from one prompt; example tool 'get_team_analytics'. Limits/cost: Pro and up. Sources: https://www.supergrow.ai/, https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing

## Kleo

- URL: https://www.kleo.so
- Positioning: Chat-first LinkedIn ghostwriter (Kleo V3 web app) bundled with templates, coaching and a creator community.
- Audience: Solo LinkedIn creators and consultants who want coaching plus an AI writer.
- Pricing: $99/mo monthly, or $999/yr annual bundle ('Normally $1,188/yr'). '$' shown, USD assumed. No free trial; annual adds 1:1 onboarding call and 30-day risk-free guarantee.

Features:

- **Chat-first drafting** (agent): Drafting starts in a chat ('What are we creating today?'). Kleo turns rough ideas into drafts and explains why a post is structured as it is (hook, angle, structure). 'Preview and edit' before publishing. Limits/cost: Unlimited post generation. Worth copying: Explaining the hook/angle/structure teaches the user while drafting. Sources: https://wf.kleo.so/, https://authoredup.com/blog/kleo-review
- **Train writing style + automatic memories** (brand): 'Train writing style' from samples of your past posts or frameworks from popular creators (vocabulary, sentence rhythm, hook style). 'Automatic memories' learn from each post you create and persist across sessions. Worth copying: Voice that keeps learning from every accepted post. Sources: https://wf.kleo.so/, https://authoredup.com/blog/kleo-review
- **Knowledge base and identity** (brand): 'Store knowledge': reusable case studies, frameworks, client wins the AI draws on. 'Define identity': who you are, what you stand for and who you address; included in every prompt. Worth copying: Separate 'facts I can cite' store from 'voice'. Sources: https://wf.kleo.so/, https://authoredup.com/blog/kleo-review
- **Swipe file + web clipper extension** (inspiration): 'Browse swipe file' of curated high-performing content; 'Save inspiration' from across the web. The Chrome extension captures posts and ideas from social media, web pages and blogs into your swipe file, where you can turn them into ideas, drafts, remixes and posts. The extension no longer acts inside LinkedIn. Limits/cost: Included in subscription. Sources: https://wf.kleo.so/, https://authoredup.com/blog/kleo-review, https://magicpost.in/comparison/kleo-versus-magicpost
- **Template, hook and prompt libraries** (format-text): 'Use templates': 160+ plug-and-play post templates, 200+ hook templates (grouped by style: curiosity, contrarian, story-opener, list-promise), 170+ top-performing post swipes, 20 viral post prompts, 20 lead-gen prompts, engagement and strategy playbooks. Sources: https://wf.kleo.so/pricing, https://authoredup.com/blog/kleo-review
- **Create graphics** (format-image): One-click visuals generated to match the post. Limits/cost: 20 graphics per month, even on the top plan. Sources: https://wf.kleo.so/, https://authoredup.com/blog/kleo-review
- **LinkedIn scheduling** (calendar): Calendar with drag-and-drop queuing; posts publish to LinkedIn at the scheduled time. Limits/cost: LinkedIn only. Previews are approximations. Sources: https://wf.kleo.so/, https://authoredup.com/blog/kleo-review
- **Multilingual generation** (format-text): Post generation in English (UK/US), German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Danish, Czech. Limits/cost: 10 languages; interface is English. Sources: https://wf.kleo.so/
- **Coaching and community bundled** (onboarding): Fast start video training, weekly live group coaching, private creator community; annual adds 1:1 onboarding call and marketing team sessions. Limits/cost: Included in $99/mo. Worth copying: Selling outcomes (coaching) with the tool justifies a high price. Sources: https://wf.kleo.so/pricing
- **Extension shut down by LinkedIn (platform risk)** (other): Kleo 1.0 was a free Chrome extension inside LinkedIn with 70,000+ users; LinkedIn required its removal (June 2025 per MagicPost; cease-and-desist per AuthoredUp review) and the team rebuilt as a paid web app (Kleo V3). Multi-profile support is 'coming soon'. Worth copying: Lesson: avoid features that automate inside LinkedIn's UI; use official APIs. Sources: https://magicpost.in/comparison/kleo-versus-magicpost, https://authoredup.com/blog/kleo-review, https://wf.kleo.so/

Sources: https://wf.kleo.so/, https://wf.kleo.so/pricing, https://authoredup.com/blog/kleo-review, https://magicpost.in/comparison/kleo-versus-magicpost

## AuthoredUp

- URL: https://authoredup.com
- Positioning: LinkedIn editor, preview and analytics for people who write their own posts; deliberately no automation.
- Audience: Hands-on LinkedIn writers, ghostwriters and agencies (pay per profile, not per seat).
- Pricing: Individual $19.95/mo monthly or $16.63/mo billed $199.5/yr; Business $14.95 per profile/mo (min 3 profiles) or $12.46/profile/mo annual; Custom Growth plan (min 10 profiles) on request. Free trial, no card (length not stated).

Features:

- **Formatting editor with multi-device preview** (editor): Write inside LinkedIn (extension) or on the web platform: bold, italic, bullet and list styles, emoji; mentions of people and companies; attachments (images, GIFs, video, docs, polls, events); 'Multi-device preview'. Limits/cost: Video, polls and events publish through the extension. Sources: https://authoredup.com/pricing, https://authoredup.com/
- **Readability grade and length insights** (editor): Shows a readability grade and character/length insights as you write; guidance suggests aiming for grade 8 or lower. Worth copying: A simple, explainable quality score on the draft. Sources: https://authoredup.com/pricing, https://authoredup.com/
- **Hooks and post-ending libraries** (format-text): Pick a hook (opening line) or ending/CTA template to insert into the post. 'Hooks library (200+ opening lines)' (pricing cards say 300+) and 'CTA & post-ending templates (150+)'. Limits/cost: Counts conflict on page. Worth copying: Insert-from-library at the exact spot in the editor. Sources: https://authoredup.com/pricing, https://authoredup.com/
- **Snippets** (editor): Save and reuse signatures, hashtags or any text block. Limits/cost: Unlimited. Sources: https://authoredup.com/pricing
- **Saved posts manager** (inspiration): Collects LinkedIn posts you saved, including ones saved before installing the extension; filter by keyword or author; export to CSV for analysis in Excel, ChatGPT or Claude. Worth copying: Imports the user's existing LinkedIn saves, so the swipe file starts full on day one. Sources: https://authoredup.com/, https://authoredup.com/pricing
- **Drafts with stages, tags and share-for-review** (compliance): Unlimited drafts; tag drafts and move them through stages 'Active', 'Ready', 'Submitted'; 'Share a draft for review' via read-only link; draft collaboration in Business. Worth copying: Read-only review link works for a compliance reviewer without a seat. Sources: https://authoredup.com/pricing
- **Calendar, reminders and best time** (calendar): 'Content calendar (profiles, pages & groups)', scheduling (through the extension), 'Reminders to post & engage', 'Best time to post (from your own posts)', activity chart for consistency, weekly goal tracker. Sources: https://authoredup.com/pricing, https://authoredup.com/
- **Smart post reuse** (remix): Suggests three past posts worth reusing, chosen by engagement rate or impressions; 'One-click reuse' opens one as a new draft. Worth copying: Evergreen recycling of the user's own winners. Sources: https://authoredup.com/pricing, https://authoredup.com/

## Patterns worth copying

Ranked by fit with the Content Studio north star (remix any proven content into the user's brand, voice, face and offer) and with Singapore financial advisers as users.

1. Remix button on every inspiration card, with the reason it worked. Taplio's 'For You' card shows 'Why is this viral?' next to 'Save' and 'Remix'; Remix streams a variation in a chat surface, then 'Open in composer', then schedule. Copy the card shape: proof, explanation, one primary action. (Taplio)
2. Remix the structure, not the words. Eden's 'Reverse Engineer' extracts hook, beats and payoff from a pasted link and 'Replicate' maps them onto the user's idea; ViralBrain's 'Apply Pattern', EasyGen's format reuse and Reepl's template tags do the same. For advisers this avoids copying a stranger's claims, which is the compliance-safe remix. (Eden, ViralBrain, EasyGen, Reepl)
3. Rank inspiration as outliers against the creator's own baseline. Eden badges posts '10x' and 'Top 1%' versus the creator's normal; Taplio sends 5 matched posts a day driven by a 5-field profile. Better signal than raw likes, and a short daily list beats a firehose. (Eden, Taplio)
4. A voice profile with a minimum sample, house rules and memory. ContentIn needs 20+ posts, Reepl 5-10; Kleo adds 'automatic memories' from every accepted post; Typefully keeps standing rules such as banned punctuation; Supergrow captures opinions and known topics, not just tone; Kleo and Copy.ai keep a separate knowledge/Infobase of citable facts. (ContentIn, Reepl, Kleo, Typefully, Supergrow, Copy.ai)
5. Interview or voice note to a week of posts. Supergrow PostCast runs a 10-15 minute AI interview ('Alex') that yields several drafts in the user's voice; Typefully, EasyGen and ViralBrain take voice memos. Advisers talk more easily than they write. (Supergrow, Typefully, EasyGen)
6. Any source to carousel with a real brand kit and an outline check first. URL, YouTube, PDF, TikTok/Reel, X thread or CSV to slides (Taplio, Contentdrips, PostNitro, aiCarousels, Reepl); PostNitro's Nitro Chat shows the outline and waits for approval before spending credits; brand kits hold several headshots so any adviser can sign; export PDF for LinkedIn, PNG/JPG for Instagram, MP4 for animated versions. (PostNitro, Contentdrips, Taplio)
7. Per-post boosts that turn engagement into leads. Taplio 'Boosts': scheduled first comment, keyword auto-reply, auto-DM to engagers (1st-degree, first 100, 24h window), auto-connect; Hypefury and Eden do comment-keyword-to-DM on Instagram; autoplugs fire only after a post crosses a threshold. (Taplio, Hypefury, Eden, Tweet Hunter)
8. Engagers become a scored CRM, and engagement happens from a focused feed. Taplio auto-builds 'Liked My Posts' and 'Commented My Posts' lists; MagicPost and ContentIn score reactors against the ICP and export CSV; Taplio's Engage feed offers Smart Replies in four intents ('Add value', 'Congratulate', 'Agree', 'Disagree') over curated or custom lists. (Taplio, MagicPost, ContentIn)
9. Approval and audit built into the board, with an AI reviewer.

# AI remix, format library and AI UGC/influencer tools

Researched 2026-10-19 from public websites, help centres and pricing pages with WebFetch only, no logins, and the full
sourced inventory sits beside this file as `remix-ugc.json` (19 competitors, 199 features, each with URLs). Prices are USD.
Unconfirmed facts say "unverified".

Coverage note: the shared web-search budget ran out partway through, so the later competitors were researched by
fetching their own pages directly. Hedra Studio plan prices and Arcads list prices could not be read from their own sites.

---

## Fastlane (usefastlane.ai), public site only

Fastlane is the closest product to the north star, and since 2026-10-14 it sells itself as a campaign agent rather than a
content tool. Its agent, Senna, learns the brand, researches the market, creates, launches and reads the results, then the
homepage tells you to "Lap again".

- Formats named on the homepage: Chalkboard, Claymation, Baby podcast, Netflix doc, Competitor remix ("AG1 remix"), Car
  yapper, Breaking news, Meme, AI podcast, Papermation, Static, Trolley remix, Pills, Text on body and Winner variation.
- One website URL produces a batch across four formats: AI UGC videos, slideshows, hook + demo videos and memes.
- Remix draws on 25,000+ trending videos tagged by niche and performance. You paste your URL, pick trends to remix,
  generate and swipe, then schedule to TikTok, and Fastlane says it reads the structure, pacing, hook style and visual
  rhythm before it "rebuilds the format around your product instead of copying the video". The homepage also shows a
  competitor static ad (AG1) turned into the brand's own version, though how that works is not public.
- The brand profile comes from one website URL. Fastlane reads the product, audience and tone, then shows them back with
  derived facts like "discount-led" and "no captions", while the research step adds personas such as Biohacker and Busy
  Professional and the longest-running rival ad.
- Faces come from a stock library of 1,000+ AI characters where "the same face fronts every video", plus 2,000+ real human
  UGC clips; according to a Kompozy review you cannot bring your own face.
- Approval is a swipe. Blitz mode works like Tinder, and nothing publishes until you approve it there.
- It posts natively to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn, and runs Meta paid ads through your own Ads
  Manager. One calendar takes the same slot or staggered times, and automations can fill it in one click.
- The Learn step compares thumbstop, ROAS, hold rate and CPA against account averages, sends winners to memory and
  auto-varies them ("Winner found. 6 variations made."), with signup and sales attribution on each post.
- Extras include a hook generator (11 hook types, 30 structures from 5 fields), warmed TikTok and Instagram accounts at $100
  a month plus $1.50 per upload, an API and MCP whose docs need JavaScript to read, and a managed service.
- Prices differ between the homepage, which shows Starter $20 (500 credits), Growth $55 (2,000), Pro $139 (10,000) and
  Enterprise $699 (50,000) with a static at 5 credits and a video at 60, and the /pricing page, which shows Free with 10
  credits, Starter $29 (250), Growth $49 (500) and Pro $149 (2,000).

## Sandcastles (sandcastles.ai)

For short-form creators who want research turned into scripts, Sandcastles is the strongest tool in the set, although it
makes no video, has no avatars and does not publish.

- Channels are found four ways (Suggested, Describe, Search, Add URL) and tracked in one watchlist across Instagram,
  TikTok and YouTube, with a "Global Sandcastles" mode that searches millions of outliers beyond your own list.
- The outlier score divides a video's views by the channel's median views over the 90 days before it was posted. So "3x"
  means three times the usual, and you can filter and sort by it next to views, engagement %, date and platform.
- One credit buys an analysis that lands in the Ideas Inbox with five fields: Content Positioning, Storytelling Format
  with "Why it Works", Contrast Mechanism, Hook Alignment, and three ways to personalise the idea for your niche. Triage is
  by keyboard, with S to shortlist, D to discard and C to turn the idea into a script.
- The script flow runs Topic, Research (editable), Hook (pick or generate) and then Script, where changes show in yellow
  and you choose "Rewrite from scratch" or "Edit script". You can start from scratch, from a saved idea, or from "Fix an
  existing script".
- Each workspace has a Persona with a content description and context of 5,000 characters each, plus a writing style of up
  to 3,000 characters that must be a pasted script, never instructions.
- Content Strategy Audit reports run on a schedule over chosen channels, and verifying your own account creates a monthly
  audit of it automatically.
- Starter is $5 for 10 credits, Pro $49 for 100, Visionary $99 for 250 and Titan $499 for 1,500. An analysis or a script
  costs 1 credit, and MCP access starts at Pro.

## Blotato (blotato.com)

Blotato remixes text posts, makes visuals from templates, and schedules to 9 platforms through an app, an API or MCP.

- Remix is one button. Under Tools > Inspiration you press "Remix" on a post and get a version that keeps "the essence
  intact", and the help page then asks you to add your own story and tidy it up in Chat. Source material can also be any of
  eight inputs: a YouTube link, TikTok, X post, article, PDF, audio file, raw text, or a live Perplexity research query.
- Visual templates include Instagram Carousel Slideshow, Image Slideshow with Text Overlays, quote cards, tweet cards,
  tutorial carousels and a "When X then Y" text slideshow. There are also avatar templates (AI Avatar with AI Generated
  B-roll, AI Selfie Talking Video with Consistent Character) and infographic scenes such as Billboard, Newspaper, Breaking
  News, Chalkboard, Manga Panel and Top Secret.
- The Brand Kit holds the offer, ideal customer, location, topics, reference images and documents. Several brands can sit
  under one account with a Primary, and visual generation has a "Use brand kit" toggle that starts off.
- Posts go to X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Bluesky and Pinterest, either now, at a set time,
  or in the "Next free slot", and weekly recurring slots are set per account.
- A shared inbox handles comments and DMs, with keyword DM automations on Instagram and Facebook.
- Analytics rank top posts by views, likes or comments and keep snapshot history, though LinkedIn is not covered.
- Starter costs $29 for 1,250 credits, Creator $97 for 5,000 and Agency $499 for 28,000. Writing is unlimited and extra
  credits cost $6 per 1,000.

## Arcads (arcads.ai)

Arcads makes AI UGC video ads and has the most explicit tooling for cloning a competitor's ad.

- It has 1,000+ actors, and you set emotion by writing it ("Surprised"); presets cover gestures, product in hand, showing
  your app, and unboxing.
- To clone yourself you record one continuous take of at least 2 minutes and Arcads trains a model on your face and voice,
  which is PRO only at 1,600 credits per video.
- You add competitors and Arcads syncs their ads from the Meta Ad Library, sortable by spend, impressions and running time.
  Ads you save as "Winning ads" are broken into a reusable structure, and the Mark agent can rebuild one for your brand or
  clone the top ad in a Meta campaign. Four Claude skills cover the same ground: spy-competitor-ads, clone-hook,
  clone-static-ad and viral-clone.
- Hook tests keep the winning body. New spoken hooks from the same actor are joined on with "Stitch Videos" for 0 credits,
  and "Replace Actor" changes only the person, so the script and scene carry over unchanged.
- Mark takes a plain-language brief and fills a Brain panel from your website. It accepts Custom Instructions of up to 2,000
  characters, runs routines daily or weekly, publishes Meta ads, and answers in Slack.
- Workflows is a node canvas; one example runs a script across 10 characters and 2 languages at once.
- Monthly credits are 8,000 on Starter, 16,000 on Creator, 48,000 on Pro, 96,000 on Scale and 192,000 on Business. A
  talking actor costs 800 credits a minute per actor. List prices show only in the app, and the one outside quote, from
  rival Creatify's blog, puts them at $110, $220 and $550 (unverified).

## Creatify (creatify.ai)

Creatify covers the whole paid-ad stack: Ad Clone, URL to video, avatars, a creative agent, a competitor tracker and a
launcher.

- Ad Clone has three entry paths. "Browse AI ad library" starts from a top Meta ad, "Start with your product" suggests ads
  from similar products and pre-brands the clone, and "Upload your own" takes any ad file. It keeps the hook pattern,
  pacing and CTA placement, rewrites the script, outputs 9:16, 16:9 and 1:1 together, and opens the result in the editor
  for 2 to 20 credits.
- The Creative Agent takes a brief or a URL and has format skills such as /ugc, /street-interview, /podcast and
  /cinematic. A vision critic checks each scene against the brief and regenerates the ones that fail, and the agent
  remembers approvals and rejections.
- Standard has 300 actors and Pro has 1,500, with 3 custom avatars on Pro.
- The Performance Agent reads Meta, Google, TikTok and Snap alongside Shopify and GA4, and a three-persona panel scores a
  cut before you spend on it.
- Free gives 10 credits, Standard is $15 for 55, Pro $55 for 280, Max $95 for 730 and Business $195 for 1,500 or more,
  and credits last two months.

## MakeUGC (makeugc.ai)

MakeUGC has the simplest remix screen found.

- Ad Remake needs only two inputs, "Reference video *" and "Product image *". You add a prompt, pick the model (Seedance
  2.0), ratio (9:16) and length (10s), and press "Remake video"; the hook, pacing and style stay while the product is
  swapped, for 100 credits.
- The Content Library sorts proven ads by category (Trending, Beauty, Fashion, Apps and more). You pick one, upload your
  product, add a description and up to 200 characters of dialog, and press "Generate Video (150 credits)".
- Style templates are Realistic, Day In The Life, Custom Review, How-to Tutorial, Unboxing, Gruns Style, Game Style, Pixar,
  Lego, Simpson, Anime, Before And After and FOOH Billboard, and a volume picker makes 1, 5 or 10 variants.
- There are 1,000+ actors, custom avatars (1 on Growth, 3 on Pro), a custom voice clone, and 50+ languages.
- Plans cost $29, $39 and $79 a month billed yearly for 500, 1,000 and 2,000 to 5,000 credits, with a $1 trial.

## HeyGen (heygen.com)

HeyGen has the easiest digital twin capture and the most polished avatars.

- An Avatar V twin needs a 15-second webcam clip and gives wide, medium and close-up shots from that one recording in 175+
  languages. HeyGen verifies the person on camera before any custom avatar is made.
- The UGC generator asks for a template or blank start, then a script, then a presenter from 1,100+ creator-style options,
  and exports 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9.
- Product Placement takes a product photo and an avatar and returns four combined images. You pick one, write up to 5,000
  characters of script, record a voice with "Voice Mirroring", describe the motion, and render at 720p or 1080p.
- Video Agent takes a 500-character prompt with a length and orientation, and previews scene by scene before the render.
- Free gives 3 videos a month; Creator is $29, Pro $49, and Business $149 plus $20 a seat.

## Captions, incl. Mirage (captions.ai)

Mirage Studio has merged into Captions, which now sells AI twins and AI editing in one app.

- An AI twin needs 10 seconds of video or a selfie, and prompt-made avatars (200+) can change outfit and background or hold
  a product.
- AI Edit applies one of 20 named styles (Prism Pro, Paper II, Impact II, Y2K, Chalk and others) to the whole video. The
  app also has chat-based editing, 100+ caption styles, eye contact correction, filler-word trimming and translation into
  100+ languages.
- Its ad generator turns a product URL into a video ad.
- Max is $24.99 for 500 credits and Frontier runs $69.99 to $279.99, with unused credits rolling over up to three times the
  monthly amount.

## Hedra (hedra.com)

Hedra's Character-3 model turns an image and an audio track into talking video of up to 10 minutes, priced from 2.5 to 6.25
cents a second. The studio is agent-led: you type "/" and choose "Create skill" to save a reusable playbook, and saved
Elements keep the same character, product and brand across generations on a shared canvas. Its UGC page fixes the product
photo and changes the person and the room on every generation, which is a cheap way to test casting. Studio plan prices
were not readable (unverified).

## Revid.ai (revid.ai)

Revid publishes the clearest list of remix intents found, on top of a very large viral library.

- The library holds about 2.77 million viral items, each stored with its transcript, hashtags, engagement and an AI
  description of the visuals.
- Seven remix options sit on every video: "Same energy, new script", "Make it about your topic", "Post the Part 2", "Same
  footage, new look", "React on camera", "Your face in it" and "Reuse the script". The writer keeps the spoken timing and
  tone and writes new words and visuals, and "Create my Version" opens the generator with a prefilled prompt.
- Deep analysis is free up to 60 a day. It covers the hook, the timed structure, the scroll-stop moment, three adaptations
  and a replication checklist.
- Automation is where Revid goes furthest. A Search & Remix worker runs on a schedule and never uses the same reference
  twice, each Auto-Mode worker makes one video a day, and a channel watcher syncs the creators you follow every day.
- One queue posts to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. Stats show on each post, and any top post can be remixed in one click.
- It costs $39 a month for Growth (2,000 credits) or $199 for Ultra (12,000 and up), and a typical 30-second video uses 40
  to 80 credits.

## Crayo (crayo.ai)

Crayo makes faceless formats such as AutoClip, Reddit Story, Fake Texts and split-screen "brainrot" videos, with 15+
subtitle styles and 100+ AI voices. Plans are $13, $27 and $55 a month billed yearly, and the public pages show no remix,
avatars or publishing.

## ReelFarm (reel.farm)

ReelFarm automates TikTok slideshows. You search Pinterest from inside the app to build "Image Collections", then set up
an automation with a hook collection and a content collection, where text size, alignment and overlay opacity are
adjustable and the defaults are 5 slides at 9:16 or 4:5. A CTA slide can be drawn at random from a collection, and the
automation publishes every day. There is also a free slideshow library and a list of 100+ hook templates with a finance
category. Plans run from Growth at $49 (100 slideshows) to Enterprise at $195, where you can bring your own Anthropic key.

## Viral.app (viral.app)

Viral.app is a creator-marketing system rather than a generator. It tracks any public account without a login, tags videos
with AI, filters a viral UGC library by outlier factor, turns research into creator briefs and pays creators, for $99 to
$499 a month.

## Virlo (virlo.ai)

Virlo runs research agents once or continuously, alerts on outliers, compares niches, and shows Meta ads next to organic
outliers for the same keywords. A Content Studio writes briefs and ad copy and a calendar publishes, with Starter at $49 and
Pro at $199.

## TopView.ai (topview.ai)

The TopView Video Agent accepts an idea, a script, a reference video or a product URL. URL to video offers ten visual
styles (Clean studio commercial, Cinematic luxury, UGC style, Street style and six more). Reference Video Recreation copies
the style, pacing and shots of a video you supply, though the steps are not public. Product Avatar puts your product in an
avatar's hand from a library of 2,000+ avatars, and plans run $36 to $56 a month with 5 to 60 free voice clones.

## Icon (icon.me, now icon.com)

Icon has moved to human UGC at $1,000 a month for 6 ads. Its 18-format library is worth copying because each format has a

Storytelling, Myth Bust / "Stop Doing X", Unboxing, B-Roll + Voiceover, Green Screen, Whiteboard Explainer, Yapping, App

## Genviral (genviral.io), found via Fastlane

Genviral pairs a viral library with a Video Cloner ("put my AI creator in this viral reel"). It also runs a slideshow
factory with 200+ templates and hosted real-device accounts, and it posts to 10 platforms with drafts and approvals. Its
open agent skill can copy an existing TikTok slideshow into an editable draft and then save that draft as a template, and
plans cost $29 to $199 a month.

## Bluma (getbluma.com), found via Fastlane

Bluma is YC-backed. According to Fastlane (unverified), you paste a TikTok, Instagram or Meta Ads URL and Bluma "de-edits"
it into scenes, text, overlays and captions so you can rebuild it with your product, and a spreadsheet view then makes bulk
variants. It only exports and costs $36 to $99 a month.

## Superscale (superscale.ai)

Superscale is a team of paid-ad agents (Creative Strategist, Script Writer, Media Buyer and more). Its Placements &

## Patterns worth copying

1. Give the Remix button an intent menu like Revid's seven options, so one viral post becomes a clear choice between
   keeping the energy, the script or the footage, making a Part 2, reacting on camera, putting your face in it, or
   changing the topic. Each option should say what is kept and what is regenerated.
2. Store a structured teardown before rebuilding anything, the way Sandcastles saves Positioning, Storytelling Format,
   Contrast Mechanism and Hook Alignment, Arcads breaks "Winning ads" into a reusable structure, and Creatify records the
   hook pattern, pace and where the CTA sits. Saved as data, one breakdown can be remixed many times.
3. Ask for two inputs and print the price on the button, as MakeUGC's Ad Remake does with a reference video, a product image,
   model, ratio and length menus, and "Generate Video (150 credits)". For an adviser the second input is their brand, offer
   and face.
4. Rank the swipe file and the Following feed by an outlier score relative to each creator, the way Sandcastles divides
   views by the creator's 90-day median, so small accounts' hits surface as remix candidates.
5. Learn the brand from a URL and keep it as memory the user can edit, as Fastlane and Arcads do (Arcads shows it in a Brain
   panel). Take the voice from a pasted sample rather than adjectives, as Sandcastles does, and put a "Use brand kit" toggle
   on every generation like Blotato.
6. Make a swipe the approval gate, as Fastlane's Blitz mode does, where nothing publishes until it has been swiped through.
   Add an automated check before the human (Creatify's vision critic) and an AI-label and disclaimer step after it
   (Superscale), which together form the compliance gate financial advisers need.
7. Return a small batch across formats from one source, the way Fastlane turns one URL or one hook into UGC, a slideshow, a
   hook + demo and a meme, and Creatify outputs three aspect ratios at once.
8. Let users keep a winning body and test only the hook, as Arcads does by stitching new spoken hooks on for 0 credits while
   Fastlane reports signups per hook. Hook-only variants are the cheapest test there is.
9. Capture a consistent face with almost no effort, from HeyGen's 15-second webcam clip or Captions' 10 seconds or selfie,
   and verify the person on camera as HeyGen does. Fastlane and Genviral keep one recurring AI creator per brand for users
   who will not appear themselves.
10. Close the loop on the user's own winners, the way Fastlane makes variations as soon as a winner appears ("6 variations
    made") and Revid remixes a top post in one click. Revid's Search & Remix worker also runs on a schedule without reusing
    a reference, while Arcads' Mark and ReelFarm run daily routines.
11. Offer a named format library with one-line definitions, like Icon's 18 formats or Fastlane's genre names (Netflix doc,
    Car yapper), so users choose by feel and each format doubles as a remix target.
12. Show a fixed price for every action before the user generates, as Arcads does for each tool, with Blotato's rule of
    charging only when a generation succeeds and Fastlane's two-number model of 5 credits for a static and 60 for a video.

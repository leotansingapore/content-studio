# A Catalyst VA runs creator ops

Status: todo
App all apps, owner VA, phase 7 (Scale what won).

## Why

Kenyon of Sideshift (3:47 to 4:32): "The first person I usually think about bringing on is just like a general manager, admin ops", someone who can "lift a lot of the day-to-day time that you spend in actually managing creators": video reviews, "sending out updates to creators on a daily basis", check-ins, pushing new formats and research, with the goal "to just buy you back time". He starts that person on a one-month pilot at "$2 to $4,000 a month" (4:32), about "$150 per creator" (5:17), plus around 5% of the bonuses creators earn, so the manager has a reason to reject weak videos. Later (36:24) he promotes top creators into recruiting, strategy or review and keeps each where they are strongest.

The plan gives Leo about three hours a week in the creator loop. A VA from the outsourcing business we already run takes the admin half of that, and Leo keeps the half that needs taste.

## What to do

1. Leo decides the start (see Open questions) and asks Catalyst for one VA at about 10 hours a week, with good written English and their own Instagram account.
2. Claude writes the job as a playbook in Catalyst's format and as a Crewhall routine list, one item per recurring task below, with its day and its definition of done. Leo reads it before the VA starts.
3. The VA's weekly tasks, all by hand:
   - Recruiting research: run the Instagram keyword searches from pilot-outside-creators, open the followers and commenters of creators who fit, and add candidates to the roster. Claude's Apify scrape and Jev's shortlist (pinned jev-1.13.0) then rank them; the VA does not judge fit.
   - Recruiting DMs: send the approved first message to the shortlist from @finternshipofficial through Meta Business Suite's inbox, with at most one follow-up, and never from Leo's personal account.
   - Calls: book each signed creator's onboarding call on Leo's booking page and put the Thursday creator call in everyone's calendar.
   - Daily update to the creator group: the week's hooks and last week's winners from the Monday read-back.
   - Late videos: one reminder per late video, within a day of the due date, then a flag to Leo in the roster instead of a second chase.
   - Payouts: by the 3rd of each month, fill the payouts table from approved videos and booked-call bonuses. Leo approves and pays. The VA never holds a payment login.
   - Thursday call notes go into the roster the same day.
4. Leo keeps the final review of every video, the choice of formats and hooks, and every payment.
5. Access through roles, never shared passwords: editor on the Lark Base roster, a person with inbox access to @finternshipofficial in Meta Business Suite, and membership of the creator group. Leo removes each when the VA rotates off.
6. After four weeks, Claude compares Leo's hours in the loop before and after from his calendar, and how many roster tasks the VA closed on time. Leo decides whether to keep the VA, add hours, or try a top creator in the role as Kenyon suggests.

## Done when

- The playbook and the Crewhall routine list exist, and Leo approved them before day one.
- For four straight weeks, every late video got one reminder within a day and every payout was prepared by the 3rd and paid by the 5th.
- No password to any account was shared; every access is a role Leo can remove.
- Leo's own time in the creator loop is measured before and after, and is lower.

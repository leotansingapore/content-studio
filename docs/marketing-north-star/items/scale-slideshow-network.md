## Why

Salim's article (x.com/rsalimx/status/2087180278930313392, section 3) puts the engine in one line: a proven format, posted daily. The accounts in it show how the product goes in. The relationship accounts name the app once, on slide 3 or 4, as something the narrator uses. Others make it one "solution" among several, or keep it in the bio. @sydneysynced reuses one winning cover and hook on post after post while the slides inside change.

Part of the article runs dozens of accounts on farmed phones, rented accounts and spoofed locations. That part stays out. TikTok's rules on fake engagement and inauthentic behaviour ban it, and a banned account takes its audience with it.

## What to do

1. Once the scout (loop-format-scout) finds a format in an app's niche, build that format's template in Postmosis.
2. Post one slideshow a day from one to three genuine accounts the app owns. The product appears once, on slide 3 or 4, as something the narrator uses.
3. Keep the winning cover and swap the slides when a post does well, the way @sydneysynced does.
4. Read the results weekly in the Monday read-back; drop the format after four weeks without a steady hit.
5. No device farms, no rented or bought accounts, no spoofed locations.

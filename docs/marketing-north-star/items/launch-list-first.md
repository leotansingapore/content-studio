## Why

Brainrot is an iPhone screen time app that its founder says makes about $400K a month (Starter Story, youtu.be/hnxn16HtLIk at 0:00). Before the app existed he told his own story on Instagram, the entrepreneur behind the scenes, and had 100K to 150K followers by launch (2:09, 3:13, 4:16). He kept the build in suspense, "I'm building an app, sign up for the waitlist", and says the launch to that list made about $10K on its first day (4:16).

Checked in public on 2 Nov 2026: his Instagram, @yoniman.mp4, has 188K followers and its bio names the app. Brainrot's own account, @thebrainrotapp, has 11.5K, about a sixteenth of his.

Leo is already on camera every week. What is missing is a launch for those reels to build toward, and a list to launch to.

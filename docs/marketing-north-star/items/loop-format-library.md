# Every short video and slideshow starts from a named format, and the read-back tags which one

Status: todo
App all apps, owner Claude, phase 2 (the loop can see).

## Why

The format book Leo downloaded on 30 Oct holds 100 short-video formats in five groups: relatable, opinion, advice, easy to film and storytelling. Salim's article (step 1) says originality is a trap at the start: build on a format that already works, and the mixing comes later from studying many of them. A named format also makes the monthly review possible, because a format can only be swapped out once posts are tagged with it.

## What we have today

Checked 30 Oct 2026.

- Postmosis's /make lists 12 formats in src/data/formats.ts (slideshow, wall of text, notification stack, static ad, meme, carousel, text post, short video script, stock montage, launch film, words over a made scene, narrated video).
- postmosis.com/vault/tiktok-video-ideas writes the book's formats up in our own words, with a template and an example for a solo professional each. The book copies other creators' captions and scripts, so only the formats are used, never their words or videos.
- On 30 Oct Leo picked a format library as a Postmosis capability.
- The Monday read-back (loop-monday-readback) does not record a post's format.

## What to do

1. In Postmosis, add the formats from the vault page to the format library as a "pick a format" step before a short-video script or a slideshow is written. Each format carries its structure, a fill-in template and one example, in our words.
2. Write passes the chosen format's structure to the writer, so the draft follows it.
3. In the read-back, Jev tags each post with its format from the same list, so a week's winners and losers are counted per format.
4. The monthly review (loop-monthly-review) reads those counts and names the format to drop and the one to try next.

## Done when

- A customer can pick a format in Postmosis and get a script that follows its structure.
- The read-back shows a format beside each post, and one monthly review has used the counts.

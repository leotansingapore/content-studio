# postmosis.com says the one thing no rival says

Status: done
App Postmosis (postmosis.com), owner Leo + Claude, phase 1 (Every booking is held).

## Why

The eight rivals in research/teardown.md each name a shelf the reader already knows. Taplio's page title calls it an "AI tool to grow on LinkedIn in 10 min/day" (taplio.com). Supergrow is a "LinkedIn Content Platform for Teams & Individuals" (supergrow.ai) and AuthoredUp an "All-in-one LinkedIn Content Creation Tool" (authoredup.com). Buffer says "Your social media workspace" (buffer.com). Later now leads with influencer campaigns (later.com) and Predis.ai with ads (predis.ai).

The teardown found two gaps. None of the eight edits the video a professional films on a phone. None speaks to a profession, because their proof is creators with followings, never an agent, a coach or a lawyer.

Shares work with the hub card position-statements, which puts one positioning sentence at the top of each app's brief. That sentence lives in product-marketing.md; this card owns the home H1 and keeps the two saying the same thing.

# Postmosis: how eight competitors get customers, and what it means for booked walkthroughs

Read on 30 Oct 2026 from public pages only. I signed up for nothing and bought nothing, and no paid API was called. Homepage and pricing facts for Taplio, Supergrow, Buffer, Later, Predis.ai and Typefully come from the 28 Oct captures in `~/Documents/Postmosis/research/competitors/`; everything else, and all of FeedHive and AuthoredUp, was fetched on 30 Oct with Scrapling (raw pages stay in the session scratchpad, not in this repo). Product Hunt pages were read through a web fetch because Scrapling got a 403. Where a number comes from someone other than the company, it says third-party. A second read the same day corrected four facts: Later's and Buffer's YouTube counts (the first read took a collaborator's count), how many of Predis.ai's ads ask for a comment, and which rivals state that no card is needed.

Postmosis is a content studio for solo professionals who post as themselves: coaches and consultants, real estate agents and financial advisers, plus freelancers, lawyers or founders. It makes carousels, edits the talking-head video they film on a phone, plans the week with reminders, and adds a link-in-bio page, an audit of their own Instagram or TikTok, and team review. There is no open sign-up and no price on the site. Every account starts with a walkthrough that Leo runs, so the number that matters here is booked walkthroughs.

The eight competitors below are the ones that reader would put beside it. Three are LinkedIn-first tools for people who post as themselves (Taplio, Supergrow, AuthoredUp). Typefully writes and schedules for X and LinkedIn. Buffer and Later are schedulers, Predis.ai makes AI posts and ads, and FeedHive schedules with AI and automation.

## Taplio

Taplio is owned by lempire ("Taplio by lempire" in its footer). Its homepage H1 is "Let AI create your next viral LinkedIn post with Taplio" and the page title calls it an "AI tool to grow on LinkedIn in 10 min/day" ([taplio.com](https://taplio.com/)). The category noun is coach: "Your AI coach for LinkedIn growth" and "One coach, the whole growth loop". The main button is "Start free trial"; the next ones are "Watch teaser video" and "Request early access" for its new assistant, Ask Taplio. The enemy it names is the blank page and the Sunday scramble ("From dreading Sundays to growing every week").

Plans are $39, $69 and $199 a month, 25% less billed yearly, with a 7-day trial of the Pro plan and a 30-day money-back guarantee on the first payment ([pricing](https://taplio.com/pricing)). The card question does not settle on public pages. The homepage FAQ says "No credit card to start". The pricing FAQ says you "must first create an account and pick a plan" and can ask for a refund "if you forget to cancel your trial", which reads like a card on file. A third-party capture of its onboarding shows 5 emails in 7 days. The first three chase setup ("Why are you benching yourself?", "Last call: Are you serious?") and the fourth, on day 3, offers 30 days for $1 ([Sequenzy](https://www.sequenzy.com/email-examples/brands/taplio/sequences/onboarding), third-party). The FAQ says Taplio runs live group demos and to register at taplio.com/contact; that page returned 404 on 30 Oct ([contact](https://taplio.com/contact)).

Its lead magnets are aimed at the LinkedIn poster's daily jobs. The free tools hub has 12 tools, including one that turns your LinkedIn profile into ready-to-post drafts, a profile feedback tool and an AI slop detector ([free tools](https://taplio.com/free-tools)). A LinkedIn benchmark built from 60,000+ posts a month opens for "One email, no password, no credit card", and the email brings a new edition each month ([benchmark](https://taplio.com/linkedin-benchmark)). The menu offers "26 free Claude Skills" to run LinkedIn from any AI ([Claude skills](https://taplio.com/claude-skills-for-linkedin)). Its academy has 5 masterclasses taught by guest creators, with a sixth coming ([masterclasses](https://academy.taplio.com/linkedin-masterclasses)); the homepage links show the guests' follower counts, such as +55k and +71k ([taplio.com](https://taplio.com/)).

The affiliate programme pays 30% for a full year on each subscription, through Rewardful, with payouts by PayPal, Stripe or direct deposit after a 30-day hold. The page tells one partner's story: a fractional CMO with 48,000 LinkedIn followers who earned $4.5k from 5,987 visitors ([affiliates](https://taplio.com/affiliates)). It recruits creators and community builders, plus consultants or podcasters.

Proof is follower counts. The homepage claims 12k+ creators and teams, "3x more reach" and "10 min a day", and every testimonial carries the writer's follower count, the largest 657,666 ([taplio.com](https://taplio.com/)). The free tools FAQ uses the team's own followers as credentials: one team member at 10K, another near 5K ([free tools](https://taplio.com/free-tools)). Taplio's single Product Hunt launch in 2022 was #1 of the day with 1,008 upvotes and #4 Marketing and Sales product of the year in that year's Golden Kitty awards; the page has 1.7K followers and 14 reviews at 4.1 ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/taplio)). The FAQ calls its Chrome extension a free companion, but the listing showed "This item is not available" on 30 Oct ([Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/dfpbcakpogbfaohnnjlgghdjkgaoiaik)); a third-party scanner marks it removed, with 60,000 users at its last count and a last update on 3 Apr 2026 ([Extension Auditor](https://extensionauditor.com/scan/taplio-x-dfpbcakpogbfaohnnjlgghdjkgaoiaik), third-party).

It is buying ads right now. The Meta Ad Library showed about 10 results, 7 of the 9 loaded from Taplio's own page, all started on 23 or 29 Oct 2026. They sell two things: Taplio as an official Claude connector ("Wait, Claude can do my LinkedIn?") and Ask Taplio ("Taplio has read every post you wrote") ([Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&q=Taplio)). The site links no YouTube channel, and the LinkedIn company page it links returned 404 when read logged out.

How a visitor becomes a customer: a free tool or the benchmark catches the email, a 7-day trial of the top plan shows everything, and a $1 first month and a 30-day refund take the risk off the first payment. Affiliates with LinkedIn audiences and a new ad campaign fill the top.

## Supergrow

Supergrow's H1 is "Turn expertise into influence on LinkedIn" and it calls itself a "LinkedIn Content Platform for Teams & Individuals" ([supergrow.ai](https://www.supergrow.ai/)). The hero splits two ways: "Start free" for professionals and "Activate my team" for marketing teams, then "See how it works". The enemy is generic AI ("AI that writes like you, not for you"), and the hook is PostCast, a 10 to 15 minute AI interview that becomes a week of posts ([supergrow.ai](https://www.supergrow.ai/)). The homepage leads with a "4.8/5 on G2" badge, a "LinkedIn Verified Partner" badge and LinkedIn B2B Institute statistics.

Pro is $39 a month ($31 billed yearly) and Teams is $199 for 5 accounts. Every plan has a 7-day trial, and the FAQ says that if you do not pick a plan "your account pauses. No surprise charges, no data loss", so no card is taken ([pricing](https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing)). The pricing page ends with "Book a demo", and Enterprise adds a dedicated onboarding call. A third-party capture shows a 4-email onboarding over 3 days, sent through Loops. The first email comes from the founders and the next two from the CEO, each with a single button: connect LinkedIn, try PostCast, try Engage, check your weekly report ([Sequenzy](https://www.sequenzy.com/email-examples/brands/supergrow/sequences/onboarding), third-party).

The affiliate programme is the most generous of the LinkedIn three. It pays 30% of every payment for as long as the customer stays, with a 60-day cookie, and pays out on the 15th once you pass $100. To recruit affiliates it prints its own trial-to-paid rate, 12% to 14%, next to the line "a small two-person team" ([affiliate](https://www.supergrow.ai/affiliate-program)). Three free tools sit in the footer, among them a post preview ([about](https://www.supergrow.ai/about-us)). Testimonials live on a Senja wall ([wall of love](https://www.supergrow.ai/wall-of-love)), which the site embeds rather than writes out.

The two founders are the reach. Deven Bhooshan (CEO) shows about 8K followers and Utsav Patel (CMO) about 12K on LinkedIn, read logged out ([Deven](https://www.linkedin.com/in/devenbhooshan), [Utsav](https://www.linkedin.com/in/utsavpm)). The company page has 5,663 followers ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/supergrowai)) and the YouTube channel 441 subscribers ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@supergrowai)). The about page claims 1200+ users in 9+ countries and 10M+ impressions generated ([about](https://www.supergrow.ai/about-us)). Its one Product Hunt launch, on 19 Feb 2024, was #1 of the day and #1 of the week with 947 upvotes ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/supergrow)). Its old Chrome extension showed "This item is not available" on 30 Oct ([Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/gjpdbigebbbdciapaflndjkbhapgpkpa)), and its pricing FAQ now says it connects through LinkedIn's official API ([pricing](https://www.supergrow.ai/pricing)). There is no AppSumo deal: the product URL redirects to the store's homepage ([AppSumo](https://appsumo.com/products/supergrow/)). On Meta it runs one 20-second video behind 8 ads, started 24 Sep 2026 ([Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&q=Supergrow)).

How a visitor becomes a customer: a no-card trial, founder-signed emails that walk one feature a day, and a demo call for teams. The visitors come from affiliates, who are paid 30% for life and told that 12% to 14% of trials convert.

## AuthoredUp

AuthoredUp calls itself an "All-in-one LinkedIn Content Creation Tool"; the H1 is "The ultimate LinkedIn content creation & analytics tool" ([authoredup.com](https://authoredup.com/)). The buttons are "Try now for free" and "Add to Chrome", with "No credit card required" under them. Its pitch is safety ("Unlike other LinkedIn tools, we do not collect your cookies", data hosted in Germany), and the proof line is "2,000+ companies & individuals trust AuthoredUp" ([authoredup.com](https://authoredup.com/)).

The Individual plan is $19.95 a month ($16.63 billed yearly), and Business is $14.95 a profile with at least 3 profiles. No card is needed for the trial, says the FAQ, though no page gives its length ([pricing](https://authoredup.com/pricing)). Onboarding by a person comes only with a custom plan of 10 profiles or more. A buyer on the Individual plan gets a 90-second demo video instead, and I found no third-party capture of its emails.

The company grew from a free beta. Its about page says the founders began in December 2021, launched a free beta in July 2022, and in 13 months gathered 30,000 beta users, spoke to 500 people and shipped 15 releases ([about](https://authoredup.com/about-us)). It describes its buyer as "NON-PROFESIONAL MARKETERS" who "just want to share their knowledge", which is close to Postmosis's reader. The Chrome extension is the product's front door: 30,000 users and 4.7 stars from 70 ratings, with a Featured badge ([Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/dkkmpkpjimkollpfgbbglcikcmgmdlhn)). There are 13 free tools with no sign-up, from a text formatter to a hiring post audit and a weekly content mix planner ([free tools](https://authoredup.com/tools)), and the footer links 8 "AuthoredUp vs" pages ([authoredup.com](https://authoredup.com/)).

Its strongest marketing page is "Made with AuthoredUp", a wall of real LinkedIn posts written in the editor, opening with the founder's own ([made with](https://authoredup.com/made-with-authoredup)). Testimonials are headed "LinkedIn Top Voices trust AuthoredUp", and several are about connecting it to Claude or ChatGPT ([authoredup.com](https://authoredup.com/)). Founder Ivana Todorovic shows about 18K LinkedIn followers ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ivanatodorovic)) and the company page 11,767 ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/authoredup)). YouTube has 538 subscribers ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@authoredup)). Its one Product Hunt launch in 2023 finished #29 of the day with 93 upvotes ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/authoredup)).

The affiliate programme pays 25% of every payment for the first 12 months. The cookie lasts 30 days, Reditus does the tracking, and payouts go by PayPal or Wise. It has three doors: referral affiliates, communities (free or paid) that get "a special discount code for members", and agencies that resell it with their LinkedIn training. Paid ads by affiliates need approval ([affiliate](https://authoredup.com/affiliate-program)). None of the first 27 results in the Meta Ad Library search for its name came from AuthoredUp ([Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&q=AuthoredUp)).

How a visitor becomes a customer: a free Chrome extension sits inside LinkedIn every day, a no-card trial opens the web app, and a wall of real posts plus LinkedIn trainers and communities with member codes do the persuading.

## Typefully

Typefully's H1 is "Write better content. Grow on social media faster", under the line "The AI-powered, collaborative social media scheduler for X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, and Threads" ([typefully.com](https://typefully.com/)). The sign-up form sits in the hero (Google, X, LinkedIn or email) beside "Join 10,000+ customers", and lower down "Get started now with our free plan". Its help pages give a bigger number, "used by 200,000+ creators" ([help](https://help.typefully.com/affiliate)).

The free plan allows 10 posts a month on one social set. Creator is "Annual billing only" and Business is billed yearly per social set ([pricing](https://typefully.com/pricing)). The price figures did not render in a headless browser on 30 Oct, so I could not read them from Typefully. A competitor's blog puts Creator at $99 a year after price checks on 12 to 14 Oct 2026 ([OpenTweet](https://opentweet.io/blog/typefully-pricing-2026), third-party and a competitor). There is no trial and no card: the free plan does that job. A third-party capture shows one welcome email, "Welcome to your new social media workspace" ([Sequenzy](https://www.sequenzy.com/email-examples/brands/typefully), third-party).

Its growth engine is built into the product. The affiliate programme pays 20% recurring with a 30-day cookie and PayPal payouts from $50 ([help](https://help.typefully.com/affiliate)). Signups that come from an affiliate's unrolled threads and Typefully Profile pages count as affiliate visits, so every shared thread is a referral link ([earn with Typefully](https://support.typefully.com/en/articles/8718327-earn-with-typefully-s-affiliate-program)). The terms ban search ads, Facebook ads and coupon sites ([terms](https://support.typefully.com/en/articles/8718340-affiliate-program-terms-of-service)). The footer links about a dozen free tools (a fake tweet generator, a character counter, six AI writing tools) and 4 comparison pages ([pricing footer](https://typefully.com/pricing)).

Product Hunt carried it. It launched three times: 693 upvotes and #3 of the day in Dec 2020, 559 and #1 of the day in Jun 2021, and 928 with #1 of the day and #2 of the week in Jun 2023, which also made it runner-up for Community and Social Product of the Year at the 2023 Golden Kitty awards. The page has 1.1K followers and 22 reviews at 4.7 ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/typefully)). The founders' channel is X, which needs a login to read; co-founder Fabrizio Rinaldi shows about 800 followers on LinkedIn ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/linuz90)). YouTube has 59 subscribers ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@typefully)). The Meta Ad Library showed "No ads match your search criteria" ([Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&q=Typefully)).

How a visitor becomes a customer: a free plan that runs out at 10 posts a month, an annual-only paid plan, and the product's own shared pages and Product Hunt launches bringing new writers in.

## Buffer

Buffer's homepage says "Your social media workspace" and the title says "Social media management for everyone" ([buffer.com](https://buffer.com/)). The hero is one email box and "Get started for free"; the closing line is "No credit card needed. Free forever." It shows a live counter, "312,992 creators, brands, and agencies using Buffer", and its open dashboard block shows a customer count of 84,741 ([buffer.com](https://buffer.com/)). It sells human support as a feature: "no bots, just real people who care".

The free plan connects 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts each. Essentials is $6 a channel a month ($5 billed yearly) and Team $12 ($10 yearly), each with a 14-day trial. If no payment details are entered by the end, the account drops to the free plan, so no card is taken. Nonprofits get 50% off ([pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing)). A third-party capture caught one activation email on day 1, "Complete your Buffer profile to start scheduling" ([Sequenzy](https://www.sequenzy.com/email-examples/brands/buffer), third-party).

Transparency is the brand. The open metrics page shows 316,903 monthly active users, $2.3M MRR and $27.9M ARR, plus the CEO's salary ([open](https://buffer.com/open)). Founder and CEO Joel Gascoigne shows about 33K LinkedIn followers ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joelgascoigne)), the company page 189,581 ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bufferapp)), and the YouTube channel 29.6K subscribers and 309 videos ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/bufferapp)).

It runs a creator community. A Discord hosts Creator Camp, a 30-day programme with daily prompts, and sprint challenges of 1 to 2 weeks between camps ([our community](https://buffer.com/our-community)); its template library is written for those sprints ([templates](https://buffer.com/templates)). Its first build contest, Buffer Commit, runs 28 Oct to 13 Nov 2026: up to 16 winners split 10% of August's net profit, a $25,600 pool, and the CEO hosted a live Zoom Q&A on 27 Oct ([Buffer Commit](https://buffer.com/api/commit)). The free tools page lists 23 tools, from hashtag generators to image resizers and a free link in bio ([free tools](https://buffer.com/free-tools)), and the footer lists 11 comparison pages ([buffer.com](https://buffer.com/)).

The partner programme pays 25% of each new customer's payments for 12 months, by application, and bans bidding on Buffer's brand ([partners](https://buffer.com/partners)). The Chrome extension has 200,000 users and 4.3 stars from 3K ratings ([Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/noojglkidnpfjbincgijbaiedldjfbhh)), and Canva is built into the composer ([Canva](https://buffer.com/integrations/canva)). Product Hunt lists 37 Buffer launches; the five shown drew 15 to 233 upvotes ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/buffer)). Meta ads not read: a search for "Buffer" returned about 12,000 results from unrelated advertisers.

How a visitor becomes a customer: a free plan that stays free, a no-card trial that falls back to it, a price per channel that grows with use, and a community that keeps people posting long enough to need more channels.

## Later

Later has moved to influencer marketing. Its H1 is "Unforgettable campaigns start with insight." and the buttons are "Start your campaign" and "Earn as a creator"; social media plans sit behind "Start free trial" in the menu ([later.com](https://later.com/)). The proof is platform scale: 20M+ creators analysed (a second copy of the same block says 16M+), 136B annual impressions from scheduled posts, 1B+ link-in-bio transactions and $2B+ in influencer-driven purchases ([later.com](https://later.com/)). Mavrck bought Later in 2022, the two merged under the Later name in 2024, and Later bought the affiliate network Mavely in 2025 ([about](https://later.com/about/)).

Social plans are $25, $50 and $110 a month, or 25% less billed yearly, each with a 14-day trial ([pricing](https://later.com/pricing/)). The scheduler page promises that the trial needs no credit card ([scheduler](https://later.com/social-media-scheduler/)), and the pricing page shows no free plan. Qualifying nonprofits get 50% off the yearly Growth plan ([pricing](https://later.com/pricing/)). A third-party capture shows only a confirmation email and "Welcome to Later Social" ([Sequenzy](https://www.sequenzy.com/email-examples/brands/later), third-party).

Its lead magnets are guides and live sessions. The resource library holds free downloadable guides, such as a day-by-day checklist to launch a social presence and "a camera-ready guide for confident video creation", next to a newsletter sign-up ([guides](https://later.com/resources/guide/)). The menu offers Expert Sessions, "Watch live and on-demand" ([later.com](https://later.com/)), and an in-person event, Later Creator Live, is set for 17 Nov 2026 in New York ([events](https://later.com/events/)). Creator Academy teaches creators to earn through Later's affiliate links, and brand campaigns "typically" want 5,000+ engaged followers ([Creator Academy](https://later.com/creator-academy-by-later/)).

The partner portal lists referral and reseller, technology (Canva among them) and nonprofit programmes, with no public commission rate ([partners](https://later.com/partners/)). YouTube has 57.1K subscribers and 752 videos ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/latermedia)), the most in this group, and LinkedIn 93,382 followers ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/latergram-me)). Product Hunt lists 9 launches; the one daily badge shown is #1 of the day on 19 Mar 2018, and the recent launches drew 3 to 156 upvotes ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/later)). Meta ads not read: a search for "Later" returned 50,000 results from unrelated advertisers.

How a visitor becomes a customer: for social plans, a no-card 14-day trial and teaching content. The company's growth effort now goes to brands buying campaigns and creators earning commissions, not to a solo professional.

## Predis.ai

Predis.ai's H1 is "The AI Ad Generator for Video Ads, UGC, & Image Ads" ([predis.ai](https://predis.ai/)). The buttons are "Try for free" and "Demo" (the demo is a page of how-to videos, not a call, [demo](https://predis.ai/live-demo/)). The homepage claims 6.4M+ users and 200M+ ads generated, "4.9/5 from 3000+ Reviews" and "Rated 4.7/5 on G2, Trustpilot, Shopify" ([predis.ai](https://predis.ai/)). An older page for coaches and creators still opens on the reader's pain: "Did You Forget to Post Today? Again?" ([coaches page](https://predis.ai/business-coach-social-media-tool/)).

It is the only one of the eight that takes a card up front. Core is $32 a month or $19 billed yearly, Rise $79 or $40, Enterprise+ $249 or $212. "We take a card to start the trial and charge it when day 3 ends", and payments after that are non-refundable ([pricing](https://predis.ai/pricing/)). The pricing page carries a savings calculator. A third-party capture shows only 2 onboarding emails in 13 days, a co-founder's welcome ("Join 100K+ Users creating content every month!") and a feature email ([Sequenzy](https://www.sequenzy.com/email-examples/brands/predis/sequences/onboarding), third-party).

Its marketing is the loudest of the eight, and it starts with 58 free tools on one hub ([free tools](https://predis.ai/free-ai-tools/)). The affiliate programme pays 30% of every payment for life through FirstPromoter. On top of that it hands affiliates bundles of $25 gift cards to give away, sends gift hampers to some, and pays $2,000 to anyone whose shout-out reaches 1M impressions in 15 days. Coaches and consultants are on its list of good fits, while brand-keyword ads are banned ([affiliate](https://predis.ai/affiliate-success-kit/)). The Meta Ad Library showed about 74 results. Of the 22 that loaded, 14 came from Predis.ai's own page and 4 more from creators' pages running ads "with Predis.ai". 8 of the 22, all 4 creator ads among them, ask viewers to comment a word to get the link by DM. Others retarget lapsed trials at $19 and $32 a month. One unrelated business's own ad carried the hashtag #madewithpredisai ([Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&q=Predis.ai)).

The three co-founders started it in 2020 ([about](https://predis.ai/about-us/)). Co-founder Tanmay Ratnaparkhe shows about 6K LinkedIn followers ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tanmay-ratnaparkhe-74395420)) and the company page 20,844 ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/predis-ai)); YouTube has 5.68K subscribers ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Predisai)). In Dec 2021 he wrote that the first Product Hunt launch "was a big mistake" because they were not ready, and that an AppSumo lifetime deal then brought $42,000 in a month, about $30,000 of it theirs after AppSumo's share, plus a Facebook group for users ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-we-made-42-000-in-1-month-from-our-appsumo-launch-b9e0f652c1), self-reported). Product Hunt lists 4 launches from 2021 to 2023; the best drew 223 upvotes and #4 of the day in May 2022 ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/predis-ai)).

How a visitor becomes a customer: comment-to-DM videos, creator ads and 58 free tools bring the visit, a 3-day trial with a card turns into a charge, and retargeting ads chase anyone who let it lapse.

## FeedHive

FeedHive's homepage announces "Introducing FeedHive v2.0" and "Social media using Automation & AI Agents"; the title calls it "AI Social Media Management & Automation" ([feedhive.com](https://feedhive.com/)). The button is "Get started for free" with "Try for free. Cancel anytime." under it, and a banner offers double AI credits for 3 months to anyone who starts now ([feedhive.com](https://feedhive.com/)). Proof is thin: a "Trusted by the best" logo row and one testimonial, with no customer counter.

Creator is EUR 19 a month, or EUR 15 billed yearly, and the top Agency plan is EUR 239 a month billed yearly. Every plan has a 7-day trial; the page does not say whether a card is needed ([pricing](https://feedhive.com/pricing)). A third-party capture shows one activation email, "Let's get FeedHive up and running" ([Sequenzy](https://www.sequenzy.com/email-examples/brands/feedhive), third-party).

It leans on search pages and its founder. The pricing page lists 12 "FeedHive vs" comparisons ([pricing](https://feedhive.com/pricing)), and the homepage menu links 6 alternatives pages ([feedhive.com](https://feedhive.com/)). There are 13 free tools that need no sign-up ([free tools](https://feedhive.com/tools)), and the footer cross-sells LinkDrip and Aidbase, two more products from the same founder. Founder Simon Hoiberg shows about 89K LinkedIn followers, with Aidbase in his profile headline ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simonhoiberg)); the company page has 3,747 ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/feedhive)) and YouTube 1.14K subscribers ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@FeedHiveHQ)). An AI-compiled profile quotes him on a podcast saying he offered his first 25 users lifetime free use ([Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/businesses/feedhive/monetization), third-party).

It sold a lifetime deal on AppSumo, now sold out, with 161 reviews at 4.8 ([AppSumo](https://appsumo.com/products/feedhive/)). Its one Product Hunt launch in 2021 drew 53 upvotes and no badge ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/feedhive)). I found no affiliate page (feedhive.com/affiliate returns 404 and the footer has none). None of the first 21 loaded Meta Ad Library results for its name came from FeedHive ([Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&q=FeedHive)).

How a visitor becomes a customer: comparison pages and free tools catch people searching for a Buffer or Later alternative, a launch bonus and a 7-day trial do the rest, and the founder's own audience carries the brand.

## What this means for Postmosis

Postmosis today, from its sitemap on 30 Oct: 9 free tools, 7 templates, 5 compare pages, 6 audience pages, 19 URLs under /guides and 34 under /vault (32 when first read; two vault pages shipped later that day), with "Book a walkthrough" as the only way in ([sitemap](https://postmosis.com/sitemap.xml)). There is no proof on the site yet, and no affiliate programme, ads or directory listings.

Four patterns run through the eight:

1. Everyone starts self-serve. All eight lead with a free plan or a trial, and only Predis.ai says it takes a card; Taplio's own pages disagree and FeedHive does not say. A person sets the account up only for bigger buyers: Supergrow's Enterprise onboarding call, AuthoredUp's 10-profile plan, Later's strategy call for brands. Supergrow's Book a demo is open to anyone, but a demo is not a setup. Taplio names live group demos, and the page they point to is a 404. A walkthrough where Leo sets it up with you is the one door none of them opens for a solo buyer.
2. The founder is the channel for the small ones. FeedHive's founder has about 89K LinkedIn followers and AuthoredUp's about 18K, while Supergrow's two have about 20K between them and sign its onboarding emails.
3. Affiliates are paid 20% to 30% recurring, and the product often carries its own referral. Typefully counts users' shared threads as affiliate visits, AuthoredUp shows a wall of posts made with it, and one business tagged #madewithpredisai in its own ad.
4. Free tools sit at the top of every funnel, from AuthoredUp's 13 to Predis.ai's 58, with comparison pages beside them.

Gaps none of them fill: none edits the video a professional films on a phone, so the brief's gap still holds. None speaks to a profession, because their proof is creators with followings, never an agent, a coach or a lawyer. And none offers a person to set it up for a one-person buyer.

The candidate moves below are sorted by how fast each could bring booked walkthroughs. Each is something one person can run. A move with no flag needs only Leo's time; the flags name anything that needs money, a third party, auth or billing.

### This week: Leo's time, no build

1. Every walkthrough ends by asking for one peer by name, who gets a setup slot the same week. Evidence: AuthoredUp's affiliate page has a tier just for referring friends and readers, and Buffer pays on any customer a referral brings, as do Taplio and Supergrow ([AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com/affiliate-program), [Buffer](https://buffer.com/partners)). Flag: billing, but only if the reward later becomes money off.
2. Leo posts as himself three times a week, with each post made in Postmosis and the walkthrough link at the end. Evidence: FeedHive's founder has about 89K LinkedIn followers ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simonhoiberg)). AuthoredUp's founder has about 18K, and her posts open its Made-with wall ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ivanatodorovic)); Taplio's FAQ even uses its team's follower counts as credentials ([free tools](https://taplio.com/free-tools)). Flag: what the founder story says is Leo's call, as the brief notes.
3. Short talking-head videos, edited in Postmosis, close with "comment WALKTHROUGH and I'll send the link", and Leo answers each comment by hand, once. Evidence: 8 of the 22 Predis.ai Meta ads that loaded, all 4 from its creators among them, end with the same comment-a-word ask ([Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&q=Predis.ai)). There are no automated DMs here, which keeps it inside Leo's standing rule.
4. A weekly 30-minute group walkthrough catches people who will not book a call alone, and each attendee is offered a 1:1 setup after. Evidence: Taplio names live group demos, but the page they point to returns 404 ([taplio.com](https://taplio.com/), [contact](https://taplio.com/contact)); Later runs live Expert Sessions ([later.com](https://later.com/)). A free video-call tool is all it takes.
5. A founding-professionals offer gives the first 20 a setup by Leo and a held price, in return for feedback and a written review. Evidence: AuthoredUp's free beta gathered 30,000 users and 500 conversations in 13 months ([about](https://authoredup.com/about-us)), while FeedHive's founder gave his first 25 users lifetime free use ([Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/businesses/feedhive/monetization), third-party). Flag: a held price needs billing, and the terms are Leo's call.
6. Every booked walkthrough gets a confirmation, a reminder and a short prep email signed by Leo, so that more of them are held. Evidence: Supergrow's founders sign a 4-email onboarding over 3 days, and Taplio sends 5 emails in 7 days to chase setup ([Supergrow](https://www.sequenzy.com/email-examples/brands/supergrow/sequences/onboarding), [Taplio](https://www.sequenzy.com/email-examples/brands/taplio/sequences/onboarding), both third-party). Flag: it needs an email sender and the booking tool's notifications, both third parties.

### This month: small builds and outreach

7. A "Made with Postmosis" page shows real posts and edited videos, and Leo's own brands fill it first. Evidence: AuthoredUp keeps a wall of posts written in its editor ([made with](https://authoredup.com/made-with-authoredup)). Flag: a customer's post goes up only with their written permission.
8. Once a month, a professional who already posts well teaches a guest session to their audience and ours. Evidence: Taplio built its academy from guest creators' masterclasses, with their follower counts on the cards ([masterclasses](https://academy.taplio.com/linkedin-masterclasses), [taplio.com](https://taplio.com/)). Flag: a third party, since the guest has to agree.
9. A 30-day posting camp sends solo professionals one prompt a day for their profession, and the last day's prompt is the walkthrough invite. Evidence: Buffer's Creator Camp runs 30 days of daily prompts in Discord, with sprints of 1 to 2 weeks between camps ([our community](https://buffer.com/our-community)). Flag: the group channel belongs to a third party, though it costs nothing.
10. One professional community a month, such as an agents' group, a coaching circle or an adviser team, gets a members-only walkthrough. Evidence: AuthoredUp gives communities a member discount code and resells through LinkedIn trainers ([affiliate](https://authoredup.com/affiliate-program)), and Taplio recruits community builders as partners ([affiliates](https://taplio.com/affiliates)). Flag: a third party, and billing if the member offer is money off.
11. Each audience page offers one download for an email, for example a phone-video checklist, which ends in the walkthrough link. Evidence: Taplio's benchmark asks for "One email, no password, no credit card" and sends a new edition every month ([benchmark](https://taplio.com/linkedin-benchmark)); Later gives away a checklist and a camera-ready video guide beside its newsletter ([guides](https://later.com/resources/guide/)). Flag: it needs an email list tool from a third party.
12. A free public version of the Instagram and TikTok audit ends with an offer to go through it with Leo. Evidence: Taplio's free tools turn a LinkedIn profile into posts and give profile feedback ([free tools](https://taplio.com/free-tools)), and AuthoredUp audits a hiring post for free ([free tools](https://authoredup.com/tools)). Flag: the audit's advice was still written for advisers on 28 Oct (product-truth), each run costs a data call from a third party, and it needs a rate limit.
13. Review links and link-in-bio pages carry a small "Made with Postmosis" link at the foot. Evidence: Typefully counts signups from users' shared threads and profiles as affiliate visits ([Typefully](https://support.typefully.com/en/articles/8718327-earn-with-typefully-s-affiliate-program)), and a business tagged #madewithpredisai in its own ad ([Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&q=Predis.ai)). 14. The first customers' words go on a testimonial wall, each with their profession and city. Evidence: Supergrow keeps a Senja wall ([wall of love](https://www.supergrow.ai/wall-of-love)), Taplio puts a follower count on every quote ([taplio.com](https://taplio.com/)) and AuthoredUp heads its quotes "LinkedIn Top Voices" ([authoredup.com](https://authoredup.com/)).

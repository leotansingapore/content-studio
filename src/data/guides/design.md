Make TikTok slideshows and Instagram carousels from a template or a blank slide.

Design is where you build a slideshow slide by slide. Every slide is a stack of layers you can move, resize and restyle, so you can match a format you've seen working.

## Start a slideshow

To start one:

1. Go to `Studio` → `Slideshows` → `Design`.
2. Pick a template under `Start from a template`, or select `New TikTok slideshow` or `Instagram carousel` for a blank one.
3. Switch between `TikTok 9:16` and `Instagram 4:5` to see each template in that size.

Design keeps your **40** newest slideshows per brand. Your own templates (from `Download` → `Save as template`) show under `Your templates`.

## Add and edit layers

The toolbar adds a layer to the slide you're on: `Text`, `Image`, `Shape` or `Fade`.

- Click a layer to pick it, drag it to move it, and drag a corner to resize it.
- Double-click a text layer to type on the slide. Press `esc` or click away when you're done.
- The design panel on the right holds everything about the picked layer: font, colour, outline, shadow, photo look and position.
- Write `[[like this]]` around words to show them in your accent colour.

A slide holds up to **60** layers and a slideshow up to **20** slides. Use the slide list on the left to add, duplicate or delete slides.

## Add a picture

Pick an image layer, then choose where the picture comes from:

- `Search`: free photos from Pexels.
- `Upload`: a picture from your computer or phone.
- `Media`: pictures saved in Media on this device, including your uploads and AI pictures.
- `Generate`: an AI picture from a description. You can make **5** a day.
- `Link`: a picture on the web. Note that some sites don't let their pictures be used, and Design tells you when that happens.

## Set up your palette

Your palette keeps your colours and fonts ready on every slide.

To set it up:

1. Select `Brand` in the toolbar.
2. Pick a colour and select `Add colour`. You can keep up to **16**.
3. Choose fonts for `Headings` and `Body text`.

New text uses your body font. `Add a heading` and `Add body text` use the fonts you picked. Your logo and headshot from your brand kit are in the same menu.

## Download or post it

Select `Download` to get your slides out:

- `This slide` or `All slides` (as a zip).
- `Choose slides` to pick which ones.
- `Send to phone` puts the slides behind a link and a QR code. Open it on your phone to share them to TikTok or Instagram. The link works for **24 hours**.

`Save to My posts` adds the slideshow to your posts with every slide attached, ready for `Schedule`.

---

**Can I undo a change?**

Yes. Use `cmd/ctrl` + `Z`, or the undo button in the toolbar. Design saves as you go.

**Can I see the tour again?**

Yes. Select `Tour` at the top of the editor.

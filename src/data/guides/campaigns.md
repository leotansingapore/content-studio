A campaign makes a new TikTok slideshow on your schedule and sends it to TikTok for you.

You set the formula once: the hooks, what the posts are about, where the pictures come from and how the words look. Each post picks a hook, writes the slides and draws them with fresh pictures.

## Who can use campaigns?

Campaigns post to TikTok, so they need posting switched on for your account. We set it up with you at your walkthrough.

## Create a campaign

To create one:

1. Go to `Studio` → `Slideshows` → `Campaigns`.
2. Select `New campaign`.
3. Under `Hook`, add a few hooks. Each post opens with one picked at random. Paste a list to add many at once.
4. Under `Content`, write what the posts are about. Select `Say it` to dictate, or `Optimize` to sharpen what you wrote.
5. Under `CTA`, choose whether the call-to-action slide goes at the end, in the middle or nowhere.

A campaign holds up to **30** hooks and **10** content slides. You can keep **20** campaigns.

Turn on `Search the web first` to have each post look up its hook before writing. Examples and posts that go to your drafts name their sources at the end of the caption; posts that go straight to your profile don't.

## Pick the pictures

Each kind of slide (hook, content, CTA) takes its pictures from a library. Choose a library from the menu under each tab, or select `New library…`.

A library can hold free Pexels photos, AI pictures, your uploads and app shots (a screenshot of your app in a phone over a scene). Go to `Studio` → `Slideshows` → `Libraries` to see and edit all of them. You can keep **30** libraries of up to **300** pictures each.

## Style the slides

- `Words` sets the font, style, colour, size and where the words sit.
- `Look on every slide` sets one photo filter and an overlay that keeps the words readable.

Select `Generate` to see an example post. Turn on `Show TikTok's safe area` to check nothing sits under TikTok's buttons. You can edit the caption and send the example to TikTok right away.

## Set where and when it posts

1. Under `Post from`, pick the TikTok account.
2. Under `Posting times`, add up to **6** times and pick the days for each.
3. Under `Posts go`, choose `To my drafts` or `Straight to my profile`.
4. Select `Start`.

Each post is made about 20 minutes before its time. If a time is missed, the campaign skips it and posts at the next one.

**Straight to my profile** posts without you seeing each one first, so you need to tick your agreement before it switches on. Until you do, posts go to your drafts. Posts that go straight out can also take TikTok's recommended music.

Each account can post **12** campaign posts and make **20** examples a day. `Campaigns` shows how many you've used today.

---

**Can I pause a campaign?**

Yes. Select `Pause` in the campaign. Posts already sent to TikTok stay there.

**What happens to my drafts if I delete a campaign?**

They stay in TikTok. Deleting a campaign removes its settings and the pictures of its posts from Postmosis.

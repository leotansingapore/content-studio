How the studio works

Why this tool exists

Most new FCs stall at the same point - Tuesday afternoon, blank page, no first sentence. Posting once a week becomes once a month, then never. Day 41 is explicit: the highest-leverage habit in your first year is consistent content, not perfect content.

Postmosis doesn't write your post for you. It removes the blank-page tax. You arrive with a topic and a real client moment; you leave 10 minutes later with three drafts to pick from, hashtags ready, an image prompt to drop into Canva. Edit into your voice. Publish.

Use it weekly. Treat it as scaffolding, not the final post.

Setup once: lock in your voice

Go to /voice. Paste 3-5 of your past posts (LinkedIn / IG / FB - whatever you already have). Hit Save. The tool distills your tone, hook patterns, and structural quirks into a voice profile that's injected into every future generation. Drafts will sound like you, not like AI-generic.

If you skip this step, drafts will read generic. Spend the 10 minutes. It's the single biggest quality lever in the tool.

Set your voice

The guided flow on /generate

The Write tab walks you through it one step at a time - Topic, Funnel, Idea, then Format. Every step has a sensible default, so you can click Next through the ones you don't care about and only tweak what matters. A progress bar up top lets you jump around.

1. Topic (pick your pillar).

Day 41 framework - Interest, Identity, Topic, Market.

  • Topic = the financial thing you want to teach (CPF SA top-ups)
  • Market = the segment you want to attract (fresh grads earning $4K)
  • Interest / Identity = social posts that humanise you

2. Funnel → 3. Idea (the spark).

Real client question, common mistake, news hook, story, before-after, "3 things you didn't know", myth-bust. Strongest posts come from a real conversation you had this week. Type the actual question into Context. Specific beats generic every time.

4. Format (where and how to post).

LinkedIn / IG / FB / TikTok · Text / Carousel / Short video / Story · DM keyword / Comment keyword / Save-share / Soft 15-min call / Open question · young-adult / working-adult / parent / pre-retiree / general.

Optional: Singlish toggle if your audience lands harder with light "lah / leh / can?".

Hit Generate 3 hooks. Pick the one you'd actually write. Three full-body drafts stream in side-by-side. Click Use this on the strongest. Edit, copy, post.

What happens after you pick a draft

  • Hashtags appear automatically (5-8 SG-relevant tags). One-click "Copy all".
  • Image prompt appears - paste into Canva / kie.ai / Midjourney for the visual.
  • Word + character counters show whether you're inside each platform's sweet spot (LinkedIn ~140 words, IG caption first 138 chars critical).
  • Compliance flags auto-scan for risky phrases ("guaranteed", "risk-free", "100% safe", "AIA is the best"). Fix before you post.
  • Auto-saved to /drafts - never lose work. Last 50 drafts kept per user.

Design slideshows

Make TikTok slideshows and Instagram carousels from a template or a blank slide.

Design is where you build a slideshow slide by slide. Every slide is a stack of layers you can move, resize and restyle, so you can match a format you've seen working.

Start a slideshow

To start one:

  1. Go to Studio → Slideshows → Design.
  2. Pick a template under Start from a template, or select New TikTok slideshow or Instagram carousel for a blank one.
  3. Switch between TikTok 9:16 and Instagram 4:5 to see each template in that size.

Design keeps your 40 newest slideshows per brand. Your own templates (from Download → Save as template) show under Your templates.

Add and edit layers

The toolbar adds a layer to the slide you're on: Text, Image, Shape or Fade.

  • Click a layer to pick it, drag it to move it, and drag a corner to resize it.
  • Double-click a text layer to type on the slide. Press esc or click away when you're done.
  • The design panel on the right holds everything about the picked layer: font, colour, outline, shadow, photo look and position.
  • Write [[like this]] around words to show them in your accent colour.

A slide holds up to 60 layers and a slideshow up to 20 slides. Use the slide list on the left to add, duplicate or delete slides.

Add a picture

Pick an image layer, then choose where the picture comes from:

  • Search: free photos from Pexels.
  • Upload: a picture from your computer or phone.
  • Media: pictures saved in Media on this device, including your uploads and AI pictures.
  • Generate: an AI picture from a description. You can make 5 a day.
  • Link: a picture on the web. Note that some sites don't let their pictures be used, and Design tells you when that happens.

Set up your palette

Your palette keeps your colours and fonts ready on every slide.

To set it up:

  1. Select Brand in the toolbar.
  2. Pick a colour and select Add colour. You can keep up to 16.
  3. Choose fonts for Headings and Body text.

New text uses your body font. Add a heading and Add body text use the fonts you picked. Your logo and headshot from your brand kit are in the same menu.

Download or post it

Select Download to get your slides out:

  • This slide or All slides (as a zip).
  • Choose slides to pick which ones.
  • Send to phone puts the slides behind a link and a QR code. Open it on your phone to share them to TikTok or Instagram. The link works for 24 hours.

Save to All posts adds the slideshow to your posts with every slide attached, ready for Schedule.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. Use cmd/ctrl + Z, or the undo button in the toolbar. Design saves as you go.

Can I see the tour again?

Yes. Select Tour at the top of the editor.

Run a slideshow campaign

A campaign makes a new TikTok slideshow on your schedule and sends it to TikTok for you.

You set the formula once: the hooks, what the posts are about, where the pictures come from and how the words look. Each post picks a hook, writes the slides and draws them with fresh pictures.

Who can use campaigns?

Campaigns post to TikTok, so they need posting switched on for your account. We set it up with you at your walkthrough.

Create a campaign

To create one:

  1. Go to Studio → Slideshows → Campaigns.
  2. Select New campaign.
  3. Under Hook, add a few hooks. Each post opens with one picked at random. Paste a list to add many at once.
  4. Under Content, write what the posts are about. Select Say it to dictate, or Optimize to sharpen what you wrote.
  5. Under CTA, choose whether the call-to-action slide goes at the end, in the middle or nowhere.

A campaign holds up to 30 hooks and 10 content slides. You can keep 20 campaigns.

Turn on Search the web first to have each post look up its hook before writing. Examples and posts that go to your drafts name their sources at the end of the caption; posts that go straight to your profile don't.

Pick the pictures

Each kind of slide (hook, content, CTA) takes its pictures from a library. Choose a library from the menu under each tab, or select New library….

A library can hold free Pexels photos, AI pictures, your uploads and app shots (a screenshot of your app in a phone over a scene). Go to Studio → Slideshows → Libraries to see and edit all of them. You can keep 30 libraries of up to 300 pictures each.

Style the slides

  • Words sets the font, style, colour, size and where the words sit.
  • Look on every slide sets one photo filter and an overlay that keeps the words readable.

Select Generate to see an example post. Turn on Show TikTok's safe area to check nothing sits under TikTok's buttons. You can edit the caption and send the example to TikTok right away.

Set where and when it posts

  1. Under Post from, pick the TikTok account.
  2. Under Posting times, add up to 6 times and pick the days for each.
  3. Under Posts go, choose To my drafts or Straight to my profile.
  4. Select Start.

Each post is made about 20 minutes before its time. If a time is missed, the campaign skips it and posts at the next one.

Straight to my profile posts without you seeing each one first, so you need to tick your agreement before it switches on. Until you do, posts go to your drafts. Posts that go straight out can also take TikTok's recommended music.

Each account can post 12 campaign posts and make 20 examples a day. Campaigns shows how many you've used today.

Can I pause a campaign?

Yes. Select Pause in the campaign. Posts already sent to TikTok stay there.

What happens to my drafts if I delete a campaign?

They stay in TikTok. Deleting a campaign removes its settings and the pictures of its posts from Postmosis.

Let Claude build slideshows in Design

With Connect Claude, Claude can start and edit your Design slideshows while you watch.

Before you start

You need a Connect Claude link. Go to Settings → Connect Claude to make one and add it to Claude.

What Claude can do

Ask Claude to make or change a slideshow. It can:

  • List the 16 templates and your slideshows.
  • Start a slideshow from a template or a blank slide.
  • Change any slide, layer or caption.

When Claude reads your brand, it gets the facts and learnings from Your voice and follows them. Each answer includes a link that opens the slideshow in Design.

Note that Claude won't start a 41st slideshow. Delete one in Design first.

Watch Claude edit

Keep the slideshow open in Design while Claude works. Its changes appear within a few seconds under Claude is editing.

  • Select Take over to stop following Claude's changes for this visit.
  • If you edited in the last few seconds, Design asks first: select Load its version to see Claude's change.

Each change Claude makes is one step you can undo.

Hard rules

  1. Never publish raw output. Cut 30% of words. Swap generic examples for one from your real client conversations. The tool is scaffolding; your voice is the differentiator.
  2. Use a real client moment in Context. Generic prompt = generic draft.
  3. The hook is the post. First 1-2 lines decide whether anyone reads further. That's why hooks-first is on by default.
  4. One post per week is the floor. Sunday ideate / Tuesday draft / Thursday publish.
